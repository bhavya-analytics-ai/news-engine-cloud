## E. Limits

- Telegram posts fire in almost every 11-minute window (see chance column): a post before a burst is the base rate, not evidence, unless its share beats chance.
- Kalshi direction: `kalshi_prior.py` holds series lists per channel, not a Nasdaq sign map, so every Kalshi move is "sign unknown".
- Bitcoin / USDJPY are 1-minute bars: lead UNKNOWN; a same-minute move can be reaction, not cause.
- A market that had no trade before the window is not measured in that window (no reference price).
- Chance sets are the same size as the burst sets (85 to 462 minutes), so chance shares on BIG carry a few points of sampling noise.
